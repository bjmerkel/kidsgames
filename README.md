# Kids Games

Fourteen games live here. From the first screen, pick one.

Emery is awesome.

## Woodland Grand Prix

A kart race with 10 woodland racers, seen from just above and behind the kart. Woody the Wood Duck is the balanced racer. The others are a rabbit, squirrel, raccoon, fox, owl, deer, hedgehog, chipmunk, and beaver. Each one feels a little different: some are fast, some turn quicker, some take longer to get going.

There are 5 tracks, three laps each: Lily Pond, Acorn Bend, Fern Switchbacks, Beaver Dam, and Starlit Hollow. The later tracks are narrower and the other racers drive faster. Stay on the road, drive over the green leaf pads to go faster, and avoid the blue puddles. Yellow boxes give an acorn, a leaf, or honey. Throw the acorn, use the leaf for a boost, or drop honey behind you.

Hold Left or Right to steer. The kart rolls on its own. Brake slows you down for a sharp turn. After all 5 races, the cup shows who scored the most points.

## Ollie Cat

A blocky skate ride with a white cat, seen from just behind the board so the street ahead is easy to follow. Each park is a street that turns, and the board rolls on its own. Tap Left or Right, the matching side of the picture, or the arrow keys, and the cat turns that way. Let go and the board eases back toward the middle. Hold a side, then let go, for a boost. Yellow stars along the street are worth points. The grass slows you down. Shift slows the board. Tap Jump, or the picture, to ollie, and slide a finger down to crouch. Another ollie in the air does a kickflip, then a grab. Crouch fits through tunnels and jumps higher. Yellow marks are speed ramps. Red marks launch you. Stay near the middle of a rail to grind it. Missing a landing or hitting a tunnel roof is a wipeout. Three wipeouts let you try that park again.

The score carries from park to park. The fifteen parks are Milk Street, Alley Rail, Tunnel Run, Gap Park, Night Bowl, Switchback, Jump Gap, Double Gap, Low Roof, Stair Drop, Overpass, Wide Canyon, Pipe Run, Sky Rail, and Midnight Final. The later parks are longer, with wider gaps, low tunnels, and rails. Rolling off a gap drops you, so ollie before the edge.

## Word Hunt

A word search for fifth grade. Each puzzle hides words from science, math, history, or reading. Tap the first letter, then the last letter. You can also drag from one to the other. The highlight snaps onto a straight line while you drag, and a near miss still counts. A colored line shows a word you found. Hint glows the first letter of a word that is still hidden.

The score carries from puzzle to puzzle. There are 20 puzzles. Each new game shuffles them inside three groups and hides the words in a new place. The first group goes across or down. The next group adds diagonals. The last group can run any straight line, including backwards, in science, math, history, and reading.

## Treasure Tide

A top-down treasure hunt with five islands. Each island is one mission, and the next island is harder. Tap Up, Down, Left, or Right, or slide a finger on the picture, to walk. Tap Look to talk, push, cut, and open. The line at the top says the next step. The heading names the island. Chart shows the map once the parrot gives it to you.

Lamp Isle: talk to the parrot, then light lamps 1, then 3, then 2. Stone Isle: push three stones onto the plates. Vine Isle: cut the vines, take the lantern, and tap the crystal. Wreck Isle: stay off the wavy water and turn the wheel four times. Fort Isle: touch marks A, then C, then B, then D, and open the beach chest. When an island is done, the Sail boat turns green. Step on it to reach the next island. The score carries. Play again starts at Lamp Isle.

## Escape Room

Five locked rooms. Tap a note to read it, then type the three numbers and tap Open. A wrong code says "Look again." and the lock stays. The score carries. A clean room is 150. A room you missed first is 80.

1. Desk Room, for a fourth grader. The notes say the three numbers.
2. Library, for a seventh grader. Divide, subtract, and count the letters in LOCK.
3. Science Lab, for a freshman. Solve for a number, finish 2, 4, 6, and use the sides of a hexagon.
4. Study, for a senior. Only one code fits every rule on the notes.
5. Archive, for a college student. Use a remainder, a power of 7, and the primes smaller than 10.

Number keys type the lock. Backspace erases one number. Enter opens the lock. Pause sits on the picture.

## Pattern Pals

A matching game for a new reader. A row of big pictures ends with an empty spot. Three big pictures sit underneath. Tap the one that comes next. A right tap says "Yes!" A wrong tap says "Look again." and the row stays. There are no lives to lose.

The score carries from row to row. A right tap on the first try is 100. A right tap after a miss is 50. There are 25 rows, and each new game deals them again. The first rows take turns with colors. The next rows take turns with shapes. Then come pairs, three pictures that repeat, and big and small. Each row has four patterns. The pictures, and which row comes first inside a group, change every game.

## Berry Burst

A garden match. Tap a berry, then tap one next to it to swap. Three or more in a line pop. Four in a line makes a striped berry that clears a row or a column. A wrapped berry clears the berries around it, and a white-centered berry clears every berry of that color. Pink jelly clears when a berry pops on it.

You have a set number of moves. The score carries from garden to garden. Running out of moves lets you try that garden again. The fifteen gardens are Sunny Patch, Berry Basket, Lemon Lane, Jelly Middles, Orchard Goal, Leaf Rows, Orange Grove, Twin Baskets, Jelly Frame, Corner Patch, High Score, Color Trio, Jelly Rows, Busy Garden, and Night Harvest. The later gardens ask for more points, more than one color, and bigger patches of jelly.

## Silk Spot

Two pictures of the same rooftop. Web Rascal, the raccoon in the red shirt and blue pants, is in both. Tap a change in either picture. A ring shows what you found. A wrong tap says to look again, and three wrong taps (or the Hint button) circles one change you have not found yet. There is no way to lose.

The score carries from picture to picture. The twenty-five pictures are Rooftop Morning, Park Swing, Night Windows, Snack Alley, Moon Nest, Chimney Puff, Kite Roof, Bakery Dusk, Star Porch, Puddle Lane, Lantern Row, Owl Windows, Flower Boxes, Flag Parade, Cat Nap, Balloon Bunch, Web Corner, Moonlit Steps, Snack Window, Nest Party, Science Fair, Planetarium, Class Garden, Field Day, and Observatory. Each new game shuffles pictures that have the same number of changes, and moves those changes to a new place. Pictures with fewer changes still come first. Science Fair, Planetarium, Class Garden, Field Day, and Observatory each hide eight changes, and several of those changes are the same kind of thing. Hard five starts on those pictures and skips the earlier ones.

## Ice Waddle

A maze chase. You play a penguin. Hold a direction to waddle. Eat every fish to finish the maze. The seals slide toward you. A big fish makes them sleepy: tag a sleepy seal and it goes home. Touching an awake seal costs a life and sends everyone back to the start. The fish you already ate stay eaten.

You have 3 lives. The score carries from maze to maze.

The five mazes are Snow Start, Ice Lanes, Fish Fort, Seal Slide, and Night Ice. The later mazes have quicker seals.

## Capy Swing

A jungle trail in the style of a classic pit runner. You play a capybara. Hold Right to run and Jump to hop. Tap the picture and the capybara jumps. A short gap is a running jump. A wide river has a vine: jump into the vine and it swings you to the far bank. Jump over the rolling logs. On Driftwood, hop across the logs in the water. Mangoes add points. The pool at the end of the trail finishes the level.

You have 3 lives. Falling in the river or touching a rolling log says try again and sends you back to the last safe bank. The score carries, and lives come back on the next trail.

The five trails are River Bend, Two Vines, Log Run, Driftwood, and Deep Jungle.

## Web Rascal

A side-scrolling rooftop game. You play a raccoon in a red shirt and blue pants. Hold Right to run and Jump to hop. A short gap is a running jump. A wide gap needs a web: jump toward the web mark, then tap Web or the mark itself. You hang there long enough to jump again. Bounce on a bug from above. Touching one from the side, or falling off the roof, costs a life. You have 3 lives. The score carries, and lives come back at the next roof.

Fifteen roofs. The first five are First Roof, Silk Gap, Bug Bounce, High Wire, and Night Nest. Then Chimney Row, Laundry Line, Fire Escape, Pigeon Parade, Clock Tower, Rain Gutter, Antenna Garden, Subway Roof, Silk Spiral, and Moon Nest. Chimneys stack up. Laundry lines and the clock tower ask for webs. Fire escapes and rain gutters ride moving planks. Pigeons are for bouncing. The moon nest is the last roof. The nest at the end of each roof finishes the level.

## Axolotl Patrol

An arcade shooter. You play a pink axolotl at the bottom of the tank. Bugs line up, then peel off and dive. Slide a finger across the tank and the axolotl follows, shooting while your finger is down. Left, Right, and Bubble sit on the sand, and the arrow keys still slide. Hold Bubble or Space to shoot. Only two bubbles can be in the air at once. Pop every bug to finish the level. Dragonflies and the king beetle drop water, so slide out of the way. You have 3 lives. A hit says try again, and losing the last life restarts that level.

The five levels are Skimmer School, Beetle Rows, Dragonfly Dive, Mixed Swarm, and King Beetle. The king takes six bubbles. The score carries from level to level.

## Pond Fishing

Each level asks for particular fish. Line the hook up with Left, Right, Up, and Down, then tap Catch. A fish that is not on the list swims away. Catch every fish the level names and the banner says you finished the level. The five ponds are Sunny Shore, Sunfish Bend, Bass Hole, Shady Bank, and Full Creel.

## Hermit Crab Crossing

A Frogger-style crossing game. You play a hermit crab. Five backyards stand between the crab and the burrow, and every one of them ends in a tunnel. Level settings, on the menu and inside the crab game, change the timer, stones, holds, cars, and swings for each yard.

Emery is awesome.

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the local address Vite prints. `npm test` runs the rules. `npm run build` typechecks and bundles the game.

## Controls

- Touch the picture. Every game paints its buttons on the picture, and the keyboard still works.
- In Hermit Crab Crossing, hold Up, Down, Left, or Right on the picture to keep hopping. Tap Tuck to pull into the shell. Arrow keys or WASD hop one step. A tucked crab is harder to clip. You cannot hop while tucked, and the tuck has to cool down.
- In Woodland Grand Prix, hold Left or Right, or the left or right side of the picture, to steer. Hold Brake to slow down. Tap Use to spend the item from a yellow box. The button name changes to Acorn, Leaf, or Honey.
- In Pond Fishing, hold the arrows on the picture to move the hook, then tap Catch for the fish the level asks for.
- In Axolotl Patrol, slide a finger across the tank and the axolotl follows and shoots. Hold Left, Right, or the arrow keys to slide. Hold Bubble or Space to shoot. Pop every diving bug.
- In Web Rascal, hold Left or Right, hold the street to run right, or tap the street to jump. Tap Web, or a web mark, to shoot a web. You hang on it, then jump.
- In Capy Swing, tap the picture to jump. Hold Left or Right, or hold the trail, to run. The Jump button works too. Jump into a vine to swing across a river, and jump over the rolling logs.
- In Ice Waddle, slide a finger across the maze to waddle. Arrow keys still work. Eat every fish. A big fish makes the seals sleepy.
- In Silk Spot, tap a change in either picture. Hint and Pause sit on the picture. H or Hint circles one change you still need.
- In Treasure Tide, tap Up, Down, Left, or Right, or slide a finger on the picture, to walk. Arrow keys walk too. Tap Look, or Space, to use what is in front of you. The top line says the next step. A green Sail boat takes you to the next island. Chart, or M, opens the map. The red mark is the next clue.
- In Escape Room, tap a note to read it. Tap the numbers, then Open. Number keys type too. Backspace erases one. Enter opens the lock. Pause sits on the picture.
- In Pattern Pals, tap the picture that comes next. The three big pictures are the choices. 1, 2, and 3 pick them too. Pause sits on the picture.
- In Berry Burst, tap a berry and then a neighbor to swap. Hint and Pause sit on the picture. H or Hint glows a pair that matches.
- In Word Hunt, tap the first letter and then the last letter, or drag between them. The line snaps to the letters, so a drag can be a little off. Hint and Pause sit on the picture. H or Hint glows a starting letter.
- In Ollie Cat, the board rolls on its own. Tap Left or Right, the matching side of the picture, or the arrow keys, and the cat turns that way. Let go and the board eases back toward the middle. Hold a side, then let go, for a boost. Steer into the yellow stars. Tap Jump to ollie, and Crouch or a downward slide to duck. Down crouches too. Shift slows the board. Ollie again in the air does a kickflip, then a grab.
- Esc, or Pause on the picture: pause. Tap the picture while paused, press Enter, or tap the big button: start, skip the intro, and continue.
- Up is toward the burrow.

## Levels

1. Emery's Driveway. The safe path is up the middle while you learn the toys.
2. Emery's Backyard. Stones drift, holds blink, and a Hot Wheels car owns the first lane until you wait.
3. Emery's Playground. Faster cars and swings that cross the middle.
4. Emery's Obstacle Course. Shorter time, quicker drift, a denser tunnel.
5. Emery's Tunnel. The tunnel is the longest part of the yard, and the last chamber runs every challenge at once.

Each level is a curb, a Hot Wheels road, stepping stones over water, swings, a climbing wall, and then the tunnel. The tunnel chambers, from the entrance to the burrow, are Hot Wheels, stepping stones, swings, climbing holds, and one combined chamber with all four. Reaching the lit burrow shows **You finished the level!** Finishing level 5 also tells you that you finished all 5 levels.

You get 4 lives, shown as shells. A hit returns you to the last safe entrance and says "Try again. Emery is awesome." The timer running out does the same. Lives refill at the next level. The score carries until a new game.

## Manual check

1. The title says Hermit Crab Crossing and Emery is awesome. The page header says it too.
2. Play level 1 by holding Up. The crab reaches the burrow without losing a life, and the banner says you finished the level.
3. On level 2, hop straight into the first lane and lose a life. Wait and cross after the car passes.
4. Tuck next to a car that only clips the legs. The shell saves you.
5. Stand on a drifting stone until it sinks, then hop off before it does.
6. Finish level 5. The banner congratulates the level and all 5 levels, and it says Emery is awesome.
