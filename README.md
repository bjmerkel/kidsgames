# Kids Games

Ten games live here. From the first screen, pick one.

Emery is awesome.

## Woodland Grand Prix

A top-down kart race with 10 woodland racers. Woody the Wood Duck is the balanced racer. The others are a rabbit, squirrel, raccoon, fox, owl, deer, hedgehog, chipmunk, and beaver. Each one feels a little different: some are fast, some turn quicker, some take longer to get going.

There are 5 tracks, three laps each: Lily Pond, Acorn Bend, Fern Switchbacks, Beaver Dam, and Starlit Hollow. The later tracks are narrower and the other racers drive faster. Stay on the road, drive over the green leaf pads to go faster, and avoid the blue puddles. Yellow boxes give an acorn, a leaf, or honey. Throw the acorn, use the leaf for a boost, or drop honey behind you.

Hold Left or Right to steer. The kart rolls on its own. Brake slows you down for a sharp turn. After all 5 races, the cup shows who scored the most points.

## Word Hunt

A word search for fifth grade. Each puzzle hides words from science, math, history, or reading. Tap the first letter, then the last letter. You can also drag from one to the other. The highlight snaps onto a straight line while you drag, and a near miss still counts. A colored line shows a word you found. Hint glows the first letter of a word that is still hidden.

The score carries from puzzle to puzzle. There are 20 puzzles. The first ones hide words across or down. Later puzzles add diagonals, then backwards words, in science, math, history, and reading.

## Berry Burst

A garden match. Tap a berry, then tap one next to it to swap. Three or more in a line pop. Four in a line makes a striped berry that clears a row or a column. A wrapped berry clears the berries around it, and a white-centered berry clears every berry of that color. Pink jelly clears when a berry pops on it.

You have a set number of moves. The score carries from garden to garden. Running out of moves lets you try that garden again. The five gardens are Sunny Patch, Berry Basket, Lemon Lane, Jelly Middles, and Orchard Goal.

## Silk Spot

Two pictures of the same rooftop. Web Rascal, the raccoon in the red shirt and blue pants, is in both. Tap a change in either picture. A ring shows what you found. A wrong tap says to look again, and three wrong taps (or the Hint button) circles one change you have not found yet. There is no way to lose.

The score carries from picture to picture. The twenty pictures are Rooftop Morning, Park Swing, Night Windows, Snack Alley, Moon Nest, Chimney Puff, Kite Roof, Bakery Dusk, Star Porch, Puddle Lane, Lantern Row, Owl Windows, Flower Boxes, Flag Parade, Cat Nap, Balloon Bunch, Web Corner, Moonlit Steps, Snack Window, and Nest Party. Each new game shuffles pictures that have the same number of changes, and moves those changes to a new place. Pictures with fewer changes still come first.

## Ice Waddle

A maze chase. You play a penguin. Hold a direction to waddle. Eat every fish to finish the maze. The seals slide toward you. A big fish makes them sleepy: tag a sleepy seal and it goes home. Touching an awake seal costs a life and sends everyone back to the start. The fish you already ate stay eaten.

You have 3 lives. The score carries from maze to maze.

The five mazes are Snow Start, Ice Lanes, Fish Fort, Seal Slide, and Night Ice. The later mazes have quicker seals.

## Capy Swing

A jungle trail in the style of a classic pit runner. You play a capybara. Hold Right to run and Jump to hop. A short gap is a running jump. A wide river has a vine: jump into the vine and it swings you to the far bank. Jump over the rolling logs. On Driftwood, hop across the logs in the water. Mangoes add points. The pool at the end of the trail finishes the level.

You have 3 lives. Falling in the river or touching a rolling log says try again and sends you back to the last safe bank. The score carries, and lives come back on the next trail.

The five trails are River Bend, Two Vines, Log Run, Driftwood, and Deep Jungle.

## Web Rascal

A side-scrolling rooftop game. You play a raccoon in a red shirt and blue pants. Hold Right to run and Jump to hop. A short gap is a running jump. A wide gap needs a web: jump toward the web mark, then tap Web or the mark itself. You hang there long enough to jump again. Bounce on a bug from above. Touching one from the side, or falling off the roof, costs a life. You have 3 lives. The score carries, and lives come back at the next roof.

The five roofs are First Roof, Silk Gap, Bug Bounce, High Wire, and Night Nest. The nest at the end of each roof finishes the level.

## Axolotl Patrol

An arcade shooter. You play a pink axolotl at the bottom of the tank. Bugs line up, then peel off and dive. Hold Left or Right to slide. Hold Bubble to shoot. Only two bubbles can be in the air at once. Pop every bug to finish the level. Dragonflies and the king beetle drop water, so slide out of the way. You have 3 lives. A hit says try again, and losing the last life restarts that level.

The five levels are Skimmer School, Beetle Rows, Dragonfly Dive, Mixed Swarm, and King Beetle. The king takes six bubbles. The score carries from level to level.

## Pond Fishing

Each level asks for particular fish. Line the hook up with Left, Right, Up, and Down, then tap Catch. A fish that is not on the list swims away. Catch every fish the level names and the banner says you finished the level. The five ponds are Sunny Shore, Sunfish Bend, Bass Hole, Shady Bank, and Full Creel.

## Hermit Crab Crossing

A Frogger-style crossing game. You play a hermit crab. Five backyards stand between the crab and the burrow, and every one of them ends in a tunnel. Level settings, on the menu and inside the crab game, change the timer, stones, holds, cars, and swings for each yard.

Emery is awesome.

## Play

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the local address Vite prints. `npm test` runs the rules. `npm run build` typechecks and bundles the game.

## Controls

- Arrow keys or WASD, or the on-screen pad: hop one step. Hold a direction to keep hopping. The picture and the pad fit the phone screen, in portrait or landscape.
- In Woodland Grand Prix, Left and Right steer, Down or Brake slows the kart, and Use spends the item from a yellow box.
- In Pond Fishing, move the hook with the arrows or the pad, then tap Catch for the fish the level asks for.
- In Axolotl Patrol, Left and Right slide the axolotl. Hold Space or Bubble to shoot. Pop every diving bug.
- In Web Rascal, Left and Right run. Space or Jump hops. F, Web, or a tap on the web mark shoots a web. You hang on it, then jump.
- In Capy Swing, Left and Right run. Space or Jump hops. Jump into a vine to swing across a river, and jump over the rolling logs.
- In Ice Waddle, the arrows or the pad waddle through the maze. Eat every fish. A big fish makes the seals sleepy.
- In Silk Spot, tap a change in either picture. H or Hint circles one change you still need.
- In Berry Burst, tap a berry and then a neighbor to swap. H or Hint glows a pair that matches.
- In Word Hunt, tap the first letter and then the last letter, or drag between them. The line snaps to the letters, so a drag can be a little off. H or Hint glows a starting letter.
- Space, or Tuck: pull into the shell. A tucked crab is harder to clip. You cannot hop while tucked, and the tuck has to cool down.
- Esc, or Pause: pause. Enter, a tap on the picture, or the big button: start, skip the intro, and continue.
- Up is toward the burrow.

## Levels

1. Emery's Driveway. The safe path is up the middle while you learn the toys.
2. Emery's Backyard. Stones drift, holds blink, and a Hot Wheels car owns the first lane until you wait.
3. Emery's Playground. Faster cars and swings that cross the middle.
4. Emery's Obstacle Course. Shorter time, quicker drift, a denser tunnel.
5. Emery's Tunnel. The tunnel is the longest part of the yard, and the last chamber runs every challenge at once.

Each level is a curb, a Hot Wheels road, stepping stones over water, swings, a climbing wall, and then the tunnel. The tunnel chambers, from the entrance to the burrow, are Hot Wheels, stepping stones, swings, climbing holds, and one combined chamber with all four. Reaching the lit burrow shows **You finished the level!** Finishing level 5 also tells you that you finished all 5 levels.

You get 4 lives, shown as shells. A hit returns you to the last safe entrance and says "Try again. Emery is awesome." The timer running out does the same. Lives refill at the next level. The score carries until a new game.

## Manual check

1. The title says Hermit Crab Crossing and Emery is awesome. The page header says it too.
2. Play level 1 by holding Up. The crab reaches the burrow without losing a life, and the banner says you finished the level.
3. On level 2, hop straight into the first lane and lose a life. Wait and cross after the car passes.
4. Tuck next to a car that only clips the legs. The shell saves you.
5. Stand on a drifting stone until it sinks, then hop off before it does.
6. Finish level 5. The banner congratulates the level and all 5 levels, and it says Emery is awesome.
